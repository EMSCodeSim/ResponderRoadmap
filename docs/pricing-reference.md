# Responder Roadmap pricing reference

Saved on October 25, 2026, before public pricing is removed during the department testing period. This is an internal reference for restoring or revising the offer after testing; it is not linked or rendered by the website.

| Plan | Public price | Active-member capacity |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Free / Crew | $0 | Up to 5 |
| Station | $299 per year | Up to 25 |
| Founding Department | $500 per year | Up to 75 |
| Department / Agency | Contact for pricing | 76+ |

An active member is a person assigned a Task Book or who signed in during the previous 90 days. The public offer described the same core training-readiness workflow across plans, with capacity as the main difference. The Founding price was described as locked while the subscription remains active. Station included no setup fee.

**Testing note:** Pricing is being hidden from the public website while departments test the product. Runtime membership/access behavior, department data, and existing subscriptions are unchanged by this website update. Live-test departments remain uncapped as configured in the application.
